Welcome to my **Digital Garden**. Unlike a traditional stream-based blog where articles are static and ordered purely by date, a digital garden is an evolving web of interconnected notes at various stages of maturity.

Ideas in this space grow organically:

1. **🌱 Seedling:** Initial thoughts, sparks, and rough working notes.
2. **🌿 Budding:** Developing concepts that have been revised with references and examples.
3. **🌲 Evergreen:** Mature, well-structured insights written to stand the test of time.
